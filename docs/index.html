Joey is a made-up character, not a real analyst. His 75% win rate is by his own count.

Forecasts are probabilities, not predictions. Avatars are party mascots (donkeys for Democrats, elephants for Republicans, eagles for independents), not likenesses. A shamrock marks a verified Notre Dame alumnus. A Y patch marks a candidate the Yale Alumni Magazine lists as a Yale alumnus. Its lists cover people in Congress and governors' offices, so a missing Y does not mean someone didn't attend Yale.
