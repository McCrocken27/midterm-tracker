★ 2026 Midterm Tracker ★

Every race. Every forecaster. One map, plus a fact-checked AI named Joey.

Joey Simone · MGT 409 final project · github.com/McCrocken27/midterm-tracker

The problem

  • "Is my race close?" means checking 10 forecaster sites, several paywalled
  • Each uses different words: Tilt, Lean, Likely, Safe
  • None tells you which races are on your ballot or how to register
  • About 10 to 15 minutes per race, so most new voters just don't

What's new

  • All 10 outlets side by side for all 506 races (vs. one opinion per site)
  • Your ballot from your address, plus the official registration link, with the address never stored
  • Prediction game: accounts, Labubu avatars, leaderboard vs. the experts
  • Ask Joey: unlike ChatGPT, every number is checked by code against real data

Closest alternatives: 270toWin consensus maps, Wikipedia ratings tables, Ballotpedia, ChatGPT
