The Problem

  • "Is my race close?" means checking 10+ forecaster sites, several paywalled. It’s hard!
  • Each uses different words: Tilt, Lean, Likely, Safe. It’s confusing!
  • None tells you which races are on your ballot or how to register. It takes effort!
  • About 10 to 15 minutes per race, so most new voters just don’t do their research! It’s too gosh darn hard to know who to vote for!!!

What’s New

  • 10 forecasting outlets side by side for all 506 races, plus sourced endorsements for 332 candidates
  • A path for newcomers: "What are the Midterms?" + Races to Watch
  • Your ballot from your address, address never stored
  • Prediction game (50 races): Labubu avatars, badges, private leagues, leaderboard vs. the experts
  • What If, Election Night, News, Quiz: all with 0 AI tokens
  • Ask Joey: every number is checked by code against real data

Closest alternatives: 270toWin consensus maps, Wikipedia ratings tables, Ballotpedia, ChatGPT

  • No emails; hashed passwords and sessions; lockout
  • Poisoned results page can't crown a fake winner
